safety

your safety comes before your crew.

rally is built on real vetting, not an open signup. here's exactly how that works, and how to stay safe once you're introduced to your crew.

how we vet

every application gets a real review

1

no bots, no open signup

a real person reviews every application before anyone's added to a group — name, age, instagram, and trip details all get checked.

2

18 and up only

rally is restricted to applicants 18 or older. age is checked at application, and reviewed again during manual approval.

3

introduced, not matched

you're grouped with people actually headed to the same event on the same dates — never randomly, never by an algorithm score.

before you meet up

a few basics worth keeping in mind

1

meet in public first

for your first time meeting your crew in person, pick a public spot — a meetup point at the venue, a restaurant, anywhere with people around.

2

tell a friend your plans

share who you're meeting and where with someone you trust, the same way you would for any trip with new people.

3

trust your gut

if something feels off before or during your trip, you don't owe anyone an explanation for stepping back. your comfort comes first.

see something, say something

if someone in your rally crew makes you uncomfortable, misrepresents themselves, or breaks your trust, tell us. we take every report seriously and can remove people from future matching.
