how it works

three steps between "i don't want to go alone" and having a crew.

1

apply to rally

tell us where you're headed and a bit about yourself. one form, two minutes, no account to build out.

2

we vet & find your overlap

every application is reviewed by a real person, then matched with others headed to the same event on the same dates.

3

get introduced, not matched

once you're approved, we send a warm intro to a small group chat. no algorithm score, no swipe deck, just people to meet up with.

your safety comes first. rally isn't an open signup — every application is personally reviewed before you're added to a group, so the people in your crew are who they say they are.
